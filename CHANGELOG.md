# Changelog

## Phase 6: playable nine-player village slice

- Added D-019 werewolf fake-seer claims for `village9`: provider-selected W-A counter-claims after genuine proactive/rescue publications, W-B follow-up results, and W-C uncontested day-start claims. The first living werewolf decides, werewolf decisions precede madman decisions, safe-silence fallbacks preserve play, and the engine fabricates all contents without blacking a werewolf or exposing fake provenance publicly. New focused coverage and minimal rescue-claim expectation updates bring the full regression suite to 862/862; `sample` remains unchanged.

- Added the optional `village9` scenario with four new NPCs and the fixed roles of two werewolves, one seer, one medium, one knight, one madman, and three citizens.
- Added private medium results after executions, ask-triggered medium claims, deterministic knight protection with no consecutive protection of the same target, and standard madman counting and divination behavior.
- Added a five-player/nine-player selector to the browser's new-game controls while preserving the default and sample five-player games.
- Added regression coverage for setup, role behavior, privacy, win conditions, and end-to-end nine-player progression.

- Fixed mention matching in `extractMentionedPlayerIds` (`src/textUtils.mjs`), the cause of a wrong-topic NPC answer in the user's 2026-11-11 real-AI playtest: asked "hanaと対決するか？" about Hana, Itsuki answered about Aoi (dead since Night 1). - Added rescue claims （土壇場CO, D-015), following the user's 2026-11-11 nine-player playtest, in which the seer died before ever claiming and power roles could be executed silently while unclaimed ("grey"). `WerewolfGame.runVote()` is now asynchronous and, before finalising an execution, checks the provisional tally: a living, not-yet-claimed seer, medium, or knight who would be executed first claims through the new pure builder `buildRescueClaim` (`src/responseGenerator.mjs`) — all not-yet-public results of their own (including results about dead players), a bare declaration if they have none, a bare declaration for the knight — and the vote is re-cast; this repeats if a different unclaimed power role becomes the provisional executee. A player-log framing line (「投票が固まる直前、Xが名乗り出た。」) precedes the claim, and only the final votes are recorded in `voteHistory` or shown as 投票結果. Registered public claims now also move suspicion at every registration path (proactive, ask-based, fabricated, rescue) via the new `applyClaimToSuspicion`: an uncontested claim lowers every other alive NPC's suspicion of the claimant by 3 and shifts suspicion by ±4 per published seer result, while a seer claim registered when another seer claim is already public is contested (both claimants +2, results ±2) — previously claims never moved other NPCs' votes at all. After a rescue seer claim that publishes results, the existing D-014 madman decision (`resolveMadmanSeerDecision`) runs before the re-vote, so the claim can be counter-claimed instead of being automatically believed; a bare result-less claim does not trigger it. Werewolves and the madman do not rescue-claim. One existing test needed the await-only update for the now-asynchronous `runVote()`. New `tests/rescueClaim.test.mjs` adds 7 cases; full regression 824/824 and `npm run sample` PASS. No live OpenAI call was made.
